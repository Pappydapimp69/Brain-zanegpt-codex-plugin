## E1 — opt in export needs aggregate counter and aggregate storage
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Project: local/sandbox-opt-in-telemetry
- Tags: [distribution][analytics][browser][gamedev][testing]
- What: A deterministic model of localStorage stats plus a copy-my-stats export, a GoatCounter-style hit counter, and the free-tier analytics limits, run against 40 simulated players to find where the zero-backend telemetry kernel holds and where it needs help.
- Why built: The localStorage-plus-opt-in-export kernel had never been applied; its claims (stats stay in the browser, the export is paste-sized, a counter is only for aggregates) were untested numbers.
- Design: A Store class with a 5,000,000-character quota that rejects writes; per-run deterministic times and deaths; two storage modes (aggregate stats object vs raw per-run log); a counter map path to count; two populations with equal run totals but best times shifted by 20 seconds; self-selected exporters swept by minimum run count; an egress counter that only the export click increments.
- Got right: The aggregate stats export stayed at most 42 characters from 10 to 5000 runs, far under a 2000-character chat paste; egress was 0 until the click and 41 characters after one click; the two populations produced the identical counter state (770 run-completed hits) while their exports differed, so the counter cannot answer per-player questions and the export can; the counter total was exact (770 of 770).
- Where/why it failed: Three of my own numeric predictions were wrong. The raw per-run log passes a 2000-character paste at 222 runs, not below 100. The 5,000,000-character quota needs about 554,078 runs at 9 characters each, not under 100,000, so quota loss only appeared once the origin was already full (157 of 200 writes lost). Self-selection bias in the export mean best time was only 3.7 to 5.3 percent with 26 to 7 of 40 players exporting and passed 10 percent (10.1) only when 3 of 40 exported, so the counter is a cross-check rather than a necessity at moderate participation.
- Fix: Store aggregates (runs, deaths, best) rather than a per-run log, keep the log, if wanted, capped to roughly 200 runs so the export remains pasteable, wrap writes in a quota check, and pair the export with a hit counter for the run-completed total when participation might be low.
- Why this fix: Aggregate size grows with digits, not runs (37 to 45 characters across 10 to 100,000 runs), while the log grows linearly at 9 characters per run; the counter is exact for totals and blind to per-player data, which is the exact complement of the opt-in export.
- Rule of thumb: In a zero-backend telemetry setup, size the export for the chat paste limit, not the storage quota: a raw log breaks a 2000-character paste at 222 runs but the quota only near 554,000, so store aggregates, and add the cookieless counter for totals because export-only means are self-selected (a bias above 10 percent appeared once only 3 of 40 players exported).
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the saved sandbox (RESULT PASS on the kernel claims, refuted numeric predictions reported). Real browser quota behaviour, real GoatCounter or Cloudflare product limits and real player behaviour were not tested; the 5,000,000-character quota and 2000-character chat limit are assumed constants of the model.
