## E1 — sandbox one sided formula explains gate invalid counts
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Project: local/sandbox-one-sided-formula-explains-gate-invalid-counts
- Tags: [ui][input][state][gamedev][testing]
- What: Deterministic sandbox showing the one-sided legality cost formula predicts the invalid-execution counts of an age-gated press policy (threshold 2) on period 15 width 5 over 600 uses.
- Why built: To re-verify from a new angle that the one-sided cost formula and the gate-versus-validity tradeoff describe the same mechanism, since the graph held them as a single-source edge.
- Design: Legal(t) = t mod 15 < 5. A hint issued at t-lag is invalid at use iff it was legal then and is illegal now (one-sided). Age gate executes any hint with lag at most 2 regardless of validity; validity fallback executes only valid hints.
- Got right: Gate invalid executions are 600 - 40*lag: 560 at lag 1 and 520 at lag 2, exactly the recorded tradeoff figures and exactly N - (N/P)*lag from the formula; validity fallback is 600/600 valid at every lag.
- Where/why it failed: My fidelity metric for lags above the threshold (400 from lag 3 upward for the gate) matched the recorded gate figure only at the plateau, and my validity-fallback fidelity (480 at lag 3, 400 at lag 5) did not reproduce the recorded 480/440/400 series, so no claim is made on that part.
- Fix: Guard by validity and filter by age only for staleness validity cannot see; predict the gate's invalid count from the one-sided formula instead of measuring it per shape.
- Why this fix: Validity is the safety property, age is a fidelity heuristic, and the formula shows the gate's invalid count grows linearly with lag until the threshold.
- Rule of thumb: Below its threshold an age gate's invalid executions equal (N/P) times the lag for one-sided legality, so predict them from the formula and keep validity as the guard.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running sb/p3.py: gate valid 560 and 520 at lags 1 and 2, validity fallback 600 at all lags; the above-threshold fidelity series was not reproduced and is left out of the claim.
