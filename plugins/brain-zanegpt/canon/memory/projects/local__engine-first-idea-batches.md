## E1 — engine first assignment caps a batch at eight ideas before an engine must repeat
- Date: 2026-10-15
- Project: local/engine-first-idea-batches
- Tags: [comedy][game-ads][ideation][sandbox]
- What: Assigning a different comic engine to each idea before choosing topics works only up to 8 ideas: engine-first round-robin keeps a 10-idea batch at a maximum of 2 per engine (10 of 10 unique trope-axis-engine triples), while topic-first random engines gave some engine 3 or more times in 76% of 10-idea batches, and any batch above 8 must repeat an engine (9 ideas gives 2, 17 gives 3). Separately, 10 cells drawn from the slot machine contain the mislabelled axis with probability 0.935.
- Why built: Train gap pick: the slot-machine kernel supplies 30 premise cells, the source-check lesson found one of its five axis labels is not the study's, and the vary-the-engine kernel says give each idea a different comic engine. This asks how far that rule scales on the slot machine's grid.
- Design: 8 engines as listed in the kernel; engine-first means round-robin assignment before cells are drawn (30 cells sampled without replacement); topic-first draws engines at random (20,000 batches, seeded); exact pigeonhole counts; a check of the 10-cell batch against the 6 of 30 cells that use the kernel's non-study axis name (exact and 20,000 simulated draws).
- Got right: The kernel's order of operations (engine first, then topic) is what keeps the batch balanced: max 2 at 10 ideas versus at least 3 in 76% of random batches. The engine-first batch also never repeats a full trope-axis-engine triple.
- Where/why it failed: The rule check repeated engine is unsatisfiable above 8 ideas by pigeonhole, so the kernel's batch check needs a cap (at most ceil(k/8) per engine), not zero repeats. Also the mislabelled axis shows up in 93.5% of 10-idea batches, so fixing the label matters more than its 20% share suggests. The 8 engines are the kernel's own list, not a measured taxonomy, and nothing here says whether viewers notice a repeated engine.
- Fix: When ideating a batch, assign engines round-robin first (cap ceil(k/8) per engine, and rotate the endpoint style too), draw cells without replacement afterwards, and use the study's verbatim axis name (popular externalization) in every cell.
- Why this fix: Round-robin is the minimum-repeat assignment, which is the best any scheme can do once k passes 8, and labelling cells from the source avoids carrying a misnamed axis into almost every batch.
- Rule of thumb: Engine-first assignment keeps a 10-idea batch at 2 per engine versus 3 or more in 76% of topic-first batches, but with 8 engines no batch over 8 can avoid a repeat, so cap repeats at ceil(k/8) instead of checking for none.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox below (simulation plus exact counts, deterministic seeds). The engine list and the cell grid come from the kernels as written; the study label comes from the source-check lesson and was not re-fetched; no viewer data.
