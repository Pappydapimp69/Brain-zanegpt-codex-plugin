## E1 — sandbox syllable slack and undercount hide crowded lines from hyphenate rule
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Project: local/sandbox-syllable-slack-and-undercount-hide-crowded-lines-from-hyphenate-rule
- Tags: [songwriting][suno-prompting][testing][tooling]
- What: Deterministic sandbox composing two Suno-specific lessons: the kernel that crowded syllables make Suno rush a line so hyphenating slows it, and the suno-builder finding that its syllable warning has a hidden slack of 3 and a counter that undercounts.
- Why built: To test whether the budget meter can actually tell a writer when the hyphenation advice applies, as the first Suno-only seam in the co-verification graph.
- Design: A vowel-group counter with silent-e handling against a 30-word hand-labelled true-syllable fixture. Budget 9 and slack 3 as in the builder. Lines built from ordinary words plus words where two vowels sit in separate syllables (fire, hour, poem, quiet, idea, radio, lion).
- Got right: The counter misses 11 of 68 true syllables and miscounts exactly the separate-vowel words. A line of fire, higher, hour, quiet, poem, lion is truly 12 syllables but counted 7, so it never warns even with slack 0. Adding words in order, the first warning fires at true 20 with slack 3 and true 18 with slack 0, against a budget of 9.
- Where/why it failed: The undercount is only 1.19 to 1 on this fixture, not the recorded 2.7 to 1, because my fixture is short and hand-picked; the direction and the mechanism reproduce, the magnitude does not.
- Fix: Show the tested threshold, count separate-vowel words with a small exception list or a pronunciation lookup, and surface the hyphenation hint from the crowded-line condition itself rather than from a late warning.
- Why this fix: The words the counter misses are the same ones a writer would hyphenate to slow the line, so the meter is blind in exactly the case the kernel exists for.
- Rule of thumb: A syllable warning tuned with slack and a naive counter stays silent on the crowded lines Suno rushes, so a hyphenate-to-slow hint must key off true syllables, not the budget meter.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running sb/s1.py on the 30-word hand-labelled fixture; the exact undercount ratio and warning positions apply to that fixture only.
