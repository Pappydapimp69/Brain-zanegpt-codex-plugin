## E1 — pitch opening joke leaves change first order intact only in uptake
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Project: local/sandbox-pitch-opening-joke-order-rule
- Tags: [humor][sandbox][pitch][presentation][model]
- What: A deterministic 10-slot model of a short pitch that adds the self-directed joke dose to the pitch ordering rule "open with the change, product later". Three orders (change-first, agenda-first, product-first) are run with and without a joke, tracking attention, warmth and a credibility belief slot by slot, then scored two ways: an uptake currency (probability the listener accepts) and a combined currency (warmth plus a payoff weight times uptake). It asks whether change-first still beats product-first once a joke is in the opening, and where the join breaks.
- Why built: Two kernels from different domains had never met: the pitch ordering rule says the opening is the change, never the product; the humor dose kernel says a joke is a dose with a credibility cost set by flaw severity and listener expectation. Opening a short pitch with a joke is the exact place they collide, because the joke occupies the slot the rule gives to the change. The sandbox models structure and stated assumptions only; every constant is assumed and nothing here measures real listeners or says anything about real people.
- Design: Beats carry a value and a dependence on a motivating frame; the change beat sets the frame in proportion to the attention absorbed, and a framed listener loses attention more slowly (0.05 vs 0.14 per slot). Absorption is attention times a credibility gate. A joke is an extra slot that pushes the last beat out, refills a fraction of the attention gap, adds warmth that satiates and scales with current belief, and adds the flaw-severity evidence term reused from the earlier dose sandbox (self mild-mix, heavy-mix, affiliative at half diagnostic weight, and an affiliative joke carried inside the change beat that blurs the frame). Contexts: casual (payoff weight 2) and stakes (15, bar 1), priors 0.40 and 0.85. Predictions were written before the first run; two post-hoc breakdowns are labelled and excluded from the RESULT line.
- Got right: With no joke the ordering held in all four cells (change-first > agenda-first > product-first; stakes/0.85 utility 13.51 vs 11.45 vs 5.91). In the uptake currency change-first beat product-first under every joke condition in every cell (24/24). In the combined currency a joke on the wrong order beat the right order with no joke in both casual cells (0.733 vs 0.541 and 2.445 vs 1.939) and in neither stakes cell. An opening self-directed joke on change-first lowered combined utility in stakes/0.40 (-0.635) and stakes/0.85 (-2.408) and raised it in casual (+0.293, +0.817). A mid-pitch joke (slot 4) absorbed more content than an opening joke in 4/4 cells. Initial attention flipped the sign of the opening joke: +1.456 absorbed content at 0.35, -1.845 at 0.95 (zero crossing between 0.55 and 0.75).
- Where/why it failed: 2 of 8 predictions failed. P4: the order margin is not additive with the joke in the stakes cells; at prior 0.40 an opening joke cut the uptake gap from 0.0702 to 0.0171 (75.6 percent), at prior 0.85 only 5.2 percent. P8, the exhaustive break grid (1536 cases, uptake only): 226 cases where product-first plus a joke beat change-first with no joke, not zero. All 226 sit in cold-audience or strong-refill cells (initial attention 0.55 or lower, or joke refill 0.6 or higher: 226 of 1152, largest uptake margin 0.392); in the attentive and modest-refill zone (initial attention 0.85 or higher and refill 0.35 or lower) there were 0 of 384. Weak change strength (0.6, 0.3) was not the main driver (violations 88, 78, 60 for 0.3, 0.6, 1.2), so the kernel's own caveat about needing a real discontinuity did not show up as the break. Also the combined-currency break is a price statement: change-first holds against any joke on product-first only above a payoff weight of 6.5 (stakes/0.40), 2.0 (stakes/0.85), 3.5 (casual/0.40) and 4.5 (casual/0.85) in warmth units, and the casual setting sits at 2.
- Fix: State the ordering rule with its currency and its audience. Keep "change first" as the default for uptake, put any joke mid-pitch in the framed region (or carry it inside the change beat) rather than before the change, and skip the opening joke when the room is already attentive. Where warmth is a goal in itself, price it against being believed before trading order for a joke: report the payoff-weight threshold, not a verdict.
- Why this fix: The order effect comes from the frame slowing attention loss, while a joke's attention refill only pays when there is a gap to refill, which an attentive room has not yet opened at slot 0; a mid-pitch slot has the gap and the frame. The joke's warmth and credibility terms are order-independent additive terms, so they leave the uptake ordering intact, but warmth sits in a different currency, which is why the same joke "breaks" the rule only when the two currencies are summed at a low payoff weight.
- Rule of thumb: In this model (not in people), a joke does not reverse the change-first ordering in the uptake currency for an attentive audience (0 of 384 grid cases), but it can in a cold audience or with a strong attention refill (226 of 1152), and in a summed warmth-plus-uptake score a joke on the wrong order beats the right order once the payoff weight is low (casual 2 against thresholds 3.5 to 4.5). So an opening joke is best placed mid-pitch, and before quoting any ordering rule to someone, ask which currency they are optimising and how attentive the room already is.
