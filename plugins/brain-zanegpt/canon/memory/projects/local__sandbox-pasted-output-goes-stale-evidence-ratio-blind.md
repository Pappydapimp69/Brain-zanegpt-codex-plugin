## E1 — pasted real output goes stale and a high evidence ratio cannot tell
- Date: 2026-10-11
- Project: local/sandbox-pasted-output-goes-stale-evidence-ratio-blind
- Tags: [presentation][credibility][research][simulation][method]
- What: A deterministic model of a technical deck whose claims about a small library are shown as prose, as pasted real output, or as a stored call that a checker re-runs, then a second library version changes three behaviors while the deck stays as it was, and one extra claim is fabricated and never run.
- Why built: The credibility kernel says to show the real artifact instead of paraphrasing it; I wanted to see how long pasted output stays evidence and whether a measure of how much evidence a deck shows can tell real from fabricated or stale.
- Design: Four real calls (price parse, rounding, split, negation) with outputs captured on version 1 and pasted, one fabricated pasted output, a prose-only deck of the same claims, a live deck re-run by a checker, and a version 2 that breaks price parsing, changes rounding at the half and trims whitespace in split. The evidence ratio is the share of claims that display a concrete artifact, used as the information-per-mark analogue from the data-ink sibling kernel.
- Got right: The evidence ratio was 1.0 for the real claims and for the fabricated one, and 0.0 for the prose deck; after version 2 a reader of the slide alone could not tell the 3 stale pasted outputs and the fabricated one from the rest (4 false claims, same as the prose deck), while a re-run flagged all 4.
- Where/why it failed: Re-running proves consistency, not correctness: when the checker re-baselines its expected outputs from version 2, it flags 0 of the 3 changed behaviors. That the checker flags the fabricated claim is by construction (it has no callable behind it), so that part is not a finding.
- Fix: Show the real artifact, but stamp it with the version and date it was captured, store the call that produced it, re-run it in the build, and keep expected outputs frozen in a place the re-run cannot overwrite.
- Why this fix: Pasted output is evidence only at capture time, and the evidence ratio is blind to provenance and freshness, so the claim has to carry what produced it and a check that fails when behavior drifts.
- Rule of thumb: In this model a pasted real output scored the same evidence ratio as a fabricated one and went stale silently when behavior changed (4 false claims invisible on the slide), so keep the producing call and capture version with each shown artifact, re-run it, and never let the re-run rewrite its own expected output.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the saved sandbox, PASS with deterministic output; the library, its changes and the reader model are hand-built and say nothing about real audiences.
