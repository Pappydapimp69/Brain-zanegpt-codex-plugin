## E1 — escalation needs matching distance
- Date: 2026-10-15
- Project: local/escalation-needs-matching-distance
- Tags: [comedy][humor][narrative][sandbox]
- What: Adding a benign-violation distance gate to the escalating-absurdity model flips its best
  schedule: without the gate, hitting the ceiling at once wins (5.11); with a fixed, grounded
  distance a mild schedule wins (1.45) and flat-at-ceiling scores 0.00; only when distance
  rises with severity does going big win again.
- Why built: Weakest-link train pick: the benign-violation kernel had no lesson applying it; the comedy-
  escalation lesson concluded habituation says hit the ceiling at once, and the dark-humor
  kernel says severe harm needs distance, so composing them tests whether that conclusion
  survives a benign requirement.
- Design: Escalation model from the lesson: habituation exp(-0.2 X), X += 1/(1 + I/5) per beat,
  response I/C, ceiling 10, six beats. Assumed gate g = exp(-((d - I/C)/0.25)^2), d =
  psychological distance. Schedules flat, linear, geometric, rule-of-three, mild; framing
  none, fixed d 0.3, or matched d = I/C.
- Got right: The escalation lesson's ranking reproduced without the gate (flat 5.11, three 3.59, linear
  2.53), and the composition behaves as both kernels say: severe beats in a grounded sketch
  fail (beats above I 6 keep at most 0.10 of their value) while mild beats survive.
- Where/why it failed: The matched case is identical to no gate by construction (g = 1 when d = I/C), so it only
  shows that a distance dial removes the gate's cost, not how hard that dial is to turn; the
  gate's shape and width (0.25) are assumptions, so only the direction of the flip is a
  finding.
- Fix: Before escalating a comedy arc to its ceiling, check the framing: in a grounded, realistic
  piece keep beats mild or let the absurdity add distance as it rises (surreal or cartoon
  framing); a constant realistic distance turns the biggest beats upsetting rather than
  funnier.
- Why this fix: Escalation raises severity, and benign violation requires the reason-it-is-okay to scale
  with severity at the same moment; distance is the cheapest benign route that scales, so it
  has to move with the beats or cap them.
- Rule of thumb: Escalate only as far as the framing's distance allows: under a fixed grounded distance the
  go-big schedule scored 0.00 and a mild one won (1.45); matched distance restores going big.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by the sandbox below; escalation model from the comedy-escalation
  lesson; the distance gate's form and width are my assumptions after McGraw et al. 2012 as
  summarised in the dark-humor kernel, so magnitudes are illustrative.
