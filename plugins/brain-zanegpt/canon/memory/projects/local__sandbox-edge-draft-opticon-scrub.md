## E1 — draft opticon scrub identity whole pipeline
- Date: 2026-08-01
- Project: local/sandbox-edge-draft-opticon-scrub
- Tags: [state][animation][gamedev][determinism]
- What: `brain train edge` surfaced the draft + opticon seam (14 shared tags). Composed pappydapimp69/draft#E7 (a scrubbable timeline's property must be a PURE FUNCTION of time — `rotAtTime(t)` closed-form, so `seek(t)` and continuous playback agree bit-identically; deliberately chosen over the per-frame accumulator pattern the same build uses for camera scale and fades) with Pappydapimp69/Opticon#E21 (a frame-rate-correct exponential smoother `1 - exp(-dt/tau)` is asymptotic and never exactly arrives; any consumer that declares its own start point inherits a jump equal to the leftover residual). Sandbox-confirmed the composed claim: draft#E7's scrub-identity guarantee is a WHOLE-PIPELINE property, not a per-term one — a single stateful exponential smoother anywhere downstream of the pure timeline silently breaks seek-vs-playback identity for the composite output, and the naive-seek mismatch dwarfs the smoother's own convergence residual (opticon#E21's quantity), which is the best any fix can achieve.
- Why built: draft#E7 records the pure-function discipline for the term it was applied to (rotation) while the SAME build keeps per-frame `smooth()` accumulators for camera scale and fades — nothing in either lesson asks whether those coexisting smoothers void the scrub guarantee the pure term paid for. opticon#E21 supplies the mechanism (asymptotic state carries path history) but was recorded as a two-motion-systems handoff bug, not connected to scrubbing at all.
- Design: Deterministic Python sandbox (fixed dt=1/60, no RNG, no wall clock). Pipeline: `rot_at_time(t)` pure closed-form (base drift + one eased kick at t=1.0, draft#E7's own shape); camera scale chases a pure target `cam_target(t)` through opticon#E21's exact smoother formula. Three paths to t=2.0: (1) continuous playback at fixed dt; (2) naive seek — timeline jumps, smoother state CARRIED (what a per-frame accumulator implementation naturally does); (3) fixed seek — smoother state re-derived on seek (snapped to its converged value).
- Got right: The sandbox reproduces both source lessons' own claims before testing the composition: the pure rotation term is bit-identical between playback and seek (draft#E7 holds, 2.200000000 both paths), and playback's smoother never exactly arrives (residual 0.0274 from target at t=2.0 — opticon#E21's asymptote).
- Where/why it failed: Naive seek's composite output diverged from playback by 0.4726 on the smoothed term (seek shows scale 1.000 where playback shows 1.4726) — a visible pop ~17x larger than the smoother's own residual — even though the timeline term was exact. The pure-function investment in one term bought nothing for the composite frame the viewer actually sees.
- Fix: On seek, every stateful smoother's state must be RE-DERIVED, not carried: snap it to its converged/closed-form value at the seek target (here `cam_target(t)`, since the target had settled). Sandbox-confirmed this bounds the seek-vs-playback mismatch to the smoother's own convergence residual (0.0274) — the theoretical floor per opticon#E21, since playback itself never exactly arrives either. (The fully-exact alternative is draft#E7's own discipline applied to every term: make the smoothed property itself a closed-form function of t.)
- Why this fix: The two lessons compose into a checklist neither states alone: draft#E7 tells you WHERE exactness comes from (closed form in t), opticon#E21 tells you WHY carrying smoother state across a jump fails (asymptotic path-history residual becomes a visible jump) — together: scrub-exactness must be audited per STAGE of the render pipeline, and any stage that keeps per-frame state needs an explicit on-seek re-derivation hook.
- Rule of thumb: A scrubbable/seekable timeline is only as scrub-exact as its LEAST pure pipeline stage — auditing the timeline function alone is not enough; grep every smoother/accumulator between it and the pixels, and give each an on-seek re-derivation (snap to converged or closed-form state). Without it, seek-vs-playback divergence is not the smoother's small residual but the full unconverged jump, which is the visible-pop case.
