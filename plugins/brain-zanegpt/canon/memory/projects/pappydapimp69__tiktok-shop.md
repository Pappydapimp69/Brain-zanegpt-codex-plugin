## E26 — combining six comparisons with six daily looks needs 15186 impressions per style 1 96x not the 2 14x product of the two corrections
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Project: Pappydapimp69/tiktok-shop
- Tags: [tiktok-shop][testing][stats]
- What: Applying both a Bonferroni correction for 6 pairwise comparisons and a repeated-look correction for 6 daily looks gives a critical z of 3.08 and 15,186 impressions per style (1.96 times the single-test 7,750), less than the 2.14 times (16,582) you get by multiplying the two separate factors (1.54 and 1.39), so a 4-style by 3-product matrix needs about 182,000 impressions and each cell takes 11 days at 1,500 impressions a day.
- Why built: The matrix budget (E13) and the daily-monitoring cost (E24) were priced separately; a seller running a style matrix and checking it daily pays both, and it was unknown whether the costs multiply.
- Design: Deterministic numerical integration (the peeking sandbox's group-sequential method) at overall alpha 0.05 divided by 6 across 6 looks, then the pooled two-proportion sample size at that critical z with power 0.80 at the final look; compared with the separate Bonferroni and Pocock sizes and their product.
- Got right: The combined z (3.08), the sample (15,186, 1.96 times), and the sub-multiplicative result (below the 16,582 product) held, and it exceeds either correction alone (11,957).
- Where/why it failed: The duration prediction was wrong: it expected each cell to finish in 7 to 9 days, but 15,186 impressions at 1,500 per day takes 11 days; power is applied at the final look only, a simplification of true group-sequential power.
- Fix: Budget a style matrix with daily monitoring at about twice the single-test traffic per style (about 15,200 impressions), not the product of the two corrections, and plan 11 days at 1,500 impressions a day.
- Why this fix: The two corrections raise the same threshold, so the combined cost is lower than their product but higher than either alone.
- Rule of thumb: If you test 4 styles and check daily, plan about 2 times the single-test impressions per style (15,186 vs 7,750), roughly 182,000 for a 4-style by 3-product matrix, rather than multiplying the two correction factors.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): First-hand run of the shipped sandbox this session: RESULT: FAIL 6 comparisons + 6 daily looks: z 3.08, 15,186 impressions per style (1.96x; naive product 2.14x), 4x3 matrix 182,232; equally spaced looks, power at the final look and 1,500 impressions per day are assumptions.
