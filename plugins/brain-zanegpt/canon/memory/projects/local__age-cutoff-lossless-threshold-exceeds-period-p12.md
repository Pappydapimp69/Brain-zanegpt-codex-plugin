## E1 — age cutoff lossless threshold exceeds period p12
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Project: local/age-cutoff-lossless-threshold-exceeds-period-p12
- Tags: [game-design][ui][input][state][gamedev][testing]
- What: Re-verified from a new angle that an age cutoff stacked on validity is lossless exactly at the largest valid-press lag, using a P12 W4 wave where lags exceed the period.
- Why built: The periodic-wave entry said the loss is zero only at a cutoff of at least the period, while the jitter entry said max lag; a wave with lags above the period separates the two readings.
- Design: Deterministic 600 ticks, no RNG. Role B available 4 of every 12 ticks; hint is B when B available at t else A; press lands at t+L, legal if B available then. Lag sets S1 {1,3,7,20}, S2 {1,2,3}, S3 {5,12,24}. Combined policy needs legality and L at most the cutoff; count presses lost versus validity alone.
- Got right: Lossless cutoffs were 20, 3 and 24 for S1, S2 and S3, i.e. the largest lag with any valid press, matching the max-lag claim and not the period.
- Where/why it failed: The period-based rule broke both ways: cutoff at the period 12 lost 400 presses in S1 and 600 in S3, while in S2 a cutoff of 3 was already lossless, well under the period.
- Fix: Set any cutoff from the measured largest valid-press lag, or omit the cutoff and rely on validity alone.
- Why this fix: Loss counts only valid presses above the cutoff, so the period is irrelevant unless it happens to bound the lag.
- Rule of thumb: Cutoff on top of validity is lossless exactly at the largest lag with a valid press, above or below the wave period; never derive it from the period.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the shipped sandbox; numbers copied from its output; one wave shape P12 W4 tested.
- Composed: local__age-cutoff-roll-up/E1
- Polarity: compatible
- Sandbox: sandboxes/2026-09-30__age-cutoff-lossless-threshold-exceeds-period-p12.py
- Prediction: S1 lossless cutoff 20 above period 12 with cutoff 12 losing presses; S2 lossless at 3 below the period; S3 lossless at 24.
- Observed: RESULT: PASS lossless cutoff = max valid lag: S1 20, S2 3, S3 24 vs period 12; period cutoff loses 400 in S1
- Scenarios: 1) S1 lag 20 above period, built to break the period rule; 2) S2 lags all below period; 3) S3 lags at multiples of the period
