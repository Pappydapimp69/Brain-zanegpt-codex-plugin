## E1 — age cutoff restricted fallback role zero valid lag
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Project: local/age-cutoff-restricted-fallback-role-zero-valid-lag
- Tags: [game-design][ui][input][state][gamedev][testing]
- What: Re-verified from a new angle that the lossless age cutoff is the largest lag with a valid press by restricting the fallback role A, which earlier sandboxes kept always available.
- Why built: With role A always available every lag has valid presses, so max lag in set and max valid lag coincide by construction; a restricted A can separate them.
- Design: Deterministic 600 ticks, no RNG, P12 with role B available when t%12<4; hint B if available else A; press at t+L legal if hinted role available at landing. Role A always, low6, low4 or never available; five lag sets; lossless cutoff found by brute-force loss count over cutoffs 0-59, independent of the max-valid-lag formula.
- Got right: Brute-force lossless cutoff matched the largest valid lag in all 5 cases; a cutoff at the period 12 lost 400, 200, 200 and 150 presses in four of them.
- Where/why it failed: Only when A was never available and lags 6 and 7 never landed in the B window did max lag in set (7) differ from the lossless cutoff (1); with periodic A the two were equal in every set, so the earlier distinction is invisible unless a lag has no valid press.
- Fix: Compute the cutoff from presses that are actually valid, not from the configured lag list or the period.
- Why this fix: Loss counts only valid presses above the cutoff, and a configured lag with no valid press adds nothing to it.
- Rule of thumb: Lossless cutoff equals the largest lag with a valid press; it equals the largest configured lag only while that lag has a valid press, which periodic fallback availability almost always guarantees.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the shipped sandbox; numbers copied from its output; one wave shape P12 W4 tested.
