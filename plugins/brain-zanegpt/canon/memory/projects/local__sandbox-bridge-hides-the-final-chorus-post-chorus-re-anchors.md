## E1 — sandbox bridge hides the final chorus post chorus re anchors
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Project: local/sandbox-bridge-hides-the-final-chorus-post-chorus-re-anchors
- Tags: [songwriting][structure][testing]
- What: Deterministic model of a listener as an order-k section predictor, run over pop forms with and without a bridge and with a post-chorus. With a bridge, the final chorus is never predicted at k = 1, 2 or 3; without a bridge it is predicted at every k. In the post-chorus form the final chorus is also unpredicted, but the post-chorus right after it is predicted, so the hook section restores predictability one section after the surprise.
- Why built: To test the structure kernel's claim that the bridge exists to stop the listener predicting how the song ends, and to see how it composes with the hooks kernel's post-chorus technique, which repeats the title right after the chorus.
- Design: sb/music/t4_bridge_predict.py. The predictor learns next-section counts from the last k sections heard so far and predicts the most common follower; no randomness. Forms: pop (V P C V P C Bridge C), the same with a third verse block instead of the bridge, post-chorus pop, and a form ending bridge then outro.
- Got right: Pop with bridge predicts 2 of 8 sections at k=1 and the final chorus is missed; pop without bridge predicts 5 of 9 and the final chorus is hit. In the post-chorus form at k=1 the hits are Pre, Chorus and Post in the second block and the final Post after the bridge.
- Where/why it failed: The bridge makes the final chorus unpredictable only because the section before it has never been heard, which is a context effect, not something about the chorus. The last section after a bridge is unpredictable whatever it is: the outro in the bridge-then-outro form is also missed at every k.
- Fix: When you want the ending withheld, put the bridge immediately before the final chorus. When you want the listener to lock back in right after that surprise, follow the final chorus with the post-chorus, whose link from the chorus is already learned.
- Why this fix: The model shows the surprise lasts exactly one section, and the next learned transition (chorus to post-chorus) re-anchors immediately, which is what a hook section is for.
- Rule of thumb: A bridge withholds only the next section (final chorus unpredicted at k 1-3, predicted with no bridge); a post-chorus after it is predicted again, so pair them when you want surprise then re-anchoring.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running sb/music/t4_bridge_predict.py; hit counts and per-section hits are observed output. That real listeners behave like an order-k predictor is a modelling assumption.
