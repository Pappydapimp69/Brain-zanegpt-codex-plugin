## E1 — learned cutoff is lossless only after the max valid lag outlier is seen
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Project: local/learned-cutoff-is-lossless-only-after-the-max-valid-lag-outlier-is-seen
- Tags: [ui][input][state][gamedev][testing]
- What: On a period-12 width-4 wave with jitter outliers on every seventh press, a cutoff learned online as the running maximum valid lag lost valid presses at every training length until the single press that carries the maximum valid lag (26, at index 104 of 140) was seen, lost 7 valid presses at 10 and 30 presses and 1 at 60 and 100, then lost none; the offline max-lag cutoff lost 0 and the period cutoff 12 lost 6 of 51.
- Why built: Train pick joining the lossless-at-max-lag results with the jitter sweep, asking what happens when the max valid lag is not known up front but learned from a prefix of the stream.
- Design: Presses at t = 3i + 1 with base lag i mod 5 plus 1 and a jitter of 6, 13 or 21 on every seventh press; valid iff the wave is available at the landing tick; 140 presses, 51 valid. Loss counted as valid presses with lag above the cutoff, for the offline max, the period, and a cutoff frozen after K presses for every K.
- Got right: The offline cutoff at the largest valid lag lost 0, the period-based cutoff lost 6, and the learned cutoff became lossless exactly at K = 105, one past the first press carrying the maximum valid lag, with loss above zero for all earlier K.
- Where/why it failed: Nothing in the prediction failed, but the result is close to true by construction: a running maximum is lossless once it equals the global maximum, so the sandbox mostly shows how late the outlier arrived here (index 104), not a new mechanism; the invalid-press side without validity was not tested.
- Fix: Do not freeze a cutoff learned from a prefix; keep it as a running maximum of valid lags, or set it from the largest lag the design permits, and re-derive it when the jitter source changes.
- Why this fix: A single late outlier lag is enough to make a frozen cutoff drop valid presses, and the loss stays until that outlier has been observed.
- Rule of thumb: A cutoff frozen from a short prefix of presses is lossless only if the prefix already contains the largest valid lag; in this stream that outlier was at press 104 of 140, so use the largest permitted lag or a running maximum instead.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox in this session on one wave (P12 W4) and one deterministic jitter pattern; it does not test gate-only behaviour or other waves.

- Picked-by: new-half
- ZaneGPT verdict: weak — the sweep fits its own prediction (PASS on one stream with one outlier position), but the "iff" is closer to a restatement of the definition of max, and the recommended running maximum still loses presses on arrival, so the rule's practical advice is not supported.
- Hidden assumption: The rule treats this one synthetic stream as if it were the general case: that the maximum valid lag shows up late and only once, and that the stream is stationary, so a running maximum can only be wrong in the "too low" direction. The rule's "use a running maximum" fix is itself the learned cutoff that lost presses until index 104, so it is not shown to be lossless. The result also depends on "valid" being decided by the wave at the landing tick, which the cutoff never sees.
- Would prove it wrong: Run the same sweep with the jitter schedule or phase shifted so the max-lag press lands at index 0–5, or with a larger outlier (say 40) injected after index 120. If the freeze-from-prefix cutoff still loses presses once the prefix contains the old maximum, or if a running maximum keeps losing presses after the first press, the "lossless iff the prefix contains the max valid lag" claim is false.
- Break scenario: Keep the 140-press stream but make a late valid press with lag 40 land on an available tick at index 135, so the max-lag press is last. Then every frozen cutoff for K ≤ 135 loses it, the "largest permitted lag" fallback is undefined unless a bound is given, and the running maximum loses that press at the moment it arrives (it can only raise the cutoff after the loss).
