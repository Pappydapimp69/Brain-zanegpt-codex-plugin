## E1 — gate only cumulative invalid grows linearly per period with slope n w p minus w over p
- Date: 2026-10-18
- Project: local/gate-only-cumulative-invalid-grows-linearly-per-period-with-slope-n-w-p-minus-w-over-p
- Tags: [gamedev][input][state][testing]
- What: Ran the lossless-cutoff-is-worst rule, the one-sided formula and the symmetric twice-ratio on lags beyond one period and found the gate-only cumulative invalid count has no plateau: it grows by exactly (N/P) x W x (P-W) for every further period of cutoff, with the symmetric count exactly twice it, and the lossless cutoff is the largest lag whose residue mod P is under W or over P-W.
- Why built: Train pick: the worst-cutoff lesson found its maximum at cutoff 9 on lags up to P-1 and the symmetric lesson had above-period lags only as a side note, so the combined cumulative cost past one period was untested.
- Design: legal(t) = (t + 3) mod P < W on P10W3, P12W5, P15W7 and P8W2 with N = 120 x P ticks (whole periods), lags 1 to 4P+3; one-sided invalid is legal at issue and illegal at use, symmetric is any change; cumulative sum over lags up to each cutoff; valid count for the lossless cutoff.
- Got right: Cumulative one-sided invalid at cutoff k x P - 1 equalled k x (N/P) x W x (P-W) in all 16 checks (for example 2520, 5040, 7560, 10080 on P10W3); symmetric equalled twice one-sided at every lag on all four shapes; the lossless cutoff matched the residue rule in all four.
- Where/why it failed: My first prediction that the lossless cutoff equals the scan limit failed on two shapes (42 of 43 on P10W3 and 33 of 35 on P8W2) because lags whose residue mod P falls between W and P-W have no valid hint at all; the residue rule replaced it and passed.
- Fix: Quote the gate-only cost of a cutoff c as (c / P) x (N/P) x W x (P-W) up to the partial period, never as a bounded maximum, and take the lossless cutoff from the residue rule, not from the scan limit.
- Why this fix: Each period of lags adds the same number of legal-to-illegal pairs, so the cost is a staircase of constant slope with no ceiling, and a cutoff chosen as lossless is then the most expensive one it could be only inside the horizon you scan.
- Rule of thumb: For periodic legality the gate-only invalid count of a cutoff grows linearly forever at (N/P) x W x (P-W) per period of cutoff, so a validity-free gate needs a hard cutoff below the period; lossless cutoffs above P cost one full slice per extra period.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox below; four period shapes, N a whole number of periods, one phase offset; partial-period windows were not run here.
