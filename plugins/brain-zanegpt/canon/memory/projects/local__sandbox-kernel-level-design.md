## E1 — the zero ability softlock audit never misses but over flags by nearly half
- Date: 2026-09-28
- Project: local/sandbox-kernel-level-design
- Tags: [level-design][graph][testing][gamedesign]
- What: Applied the ideas kernel that every one-way drop in an ability-gated level should be audited by BFS assuming the player holds zero abilities. Against an exact search over (room, abilities) states the audit missed nothing, but nearly half its flags were false alarms.
- Why built: brain train gap listed the kernel as unapplied. sandbox-leveldesign-softlock/E1 verified the audit on one five-room graph; its miss and false-alarm rates on varied levels were unknown.
- Design: 3000 seeded levels of 10 rooms: a random tree of two-way edges plus 3 extra, 40% gated by one of 3 abilities, plus 2 one-way drops (30% gated), 3 ability pickups and a goal room. 2438 were completable from spawn. Ground truth: a softlock exists if any reachable (room, abilities) state cannot reach the goal. The audit flags a level if any drop whose origin room is reachable has a landing room from which, starting with zero abilities and collecting pickups, the goal is unreachable.
- Got right: Scoring the audit against an exact state search instead of against hand-picked levels, which is the only way to count false alarms.
- Where/why it failed: The audit caught all 327 softlocked levels and missed 0: starting with zero abilities is the worst case, and abilities only accumulate. It also flagged 257 levels that had no softlock, 44% of its 584 flags, where every real route to the drop already required the ability that escapes the landing room.
- Fix: Keep the zero-ability audit as a cheap first pass that cannot miss, then check each flag with a search over (room, abilities) states, starting from the ability sets a player can actually hold at the drop's origin.
- Why this fix: The zero-ability assumption is what makes the audit sound; it also makes it pessimistic about drops that are only reachable once the player has the escape ability.
- Rule of thumb: A zero-ability BFS audit of one-way drops missed 0 of 327 softlocked levels but raised 257 false alarms (44% of its flags) on levels where every route to the drop already required the escape ability; confirm each flag with a (room, abilities) state search from the ability sets actually held at the drop.

Observed over 2438 completable levels: 327 caught, 257 false alarms, 0 missed, 1854 clean. The level generator is synthetic; authored Metroidvania maps will have a different false-alarm share.
