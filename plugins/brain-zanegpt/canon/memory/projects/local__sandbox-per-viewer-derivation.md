## E1 — per viewer derivation reveal needs a lucid rendered panel
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Project: local/sandbox-per-viewer-derivation
- Tags: [design][state][npc]
- What: Deterministic model of a shared inventory (6 slots, 2 phantoms) viewed through per-viewer derivation, testing when the divergence between a fooled and a lucid reading actually becomes a reveal. A reveal happened only when a lucid viewer had a rendered, on-screen panel and the other viewer was fooled on that slot; NPC observers, shared delusions and unrendered panels gave zero reveals.
- Why built: The kernel claims per-viewer derivation gives asymmetry but not a reveal when the observer is an NPC with no panel. Train pass asked to push it until it stops holding, since the boundary is the lesson.
- Design: One shared inventory array of {real, kind, claimed}. perceived(fooled_set, sim) derives each viewer's reading. derivation_reveals counts a slot only when two RENDERED panels disagree (one real, one phantom). offer_verb models the explicit hand-over: the holder offers each slot it believes real, the receiver checks it against its own reading, one verb spent per offer. Six scenarios, three built to break the claim (shared delusion, unrendered lucid viewer, fooled NPC receiver).
- Got right: Two humans fooled on {2,4} and {4} revealed exactly slot 2 with zero verbs (slot 4 is a shared delusion and stays hidden). With a lucid NPC and no panel, derivation revealed nothing while an offer verb revealed both phantom slots for exactly 2 verbs. A lucid actor confirmed only the 4 real slots, so the second-actor sibling kernel composes with it.
- Where/why it failed: The "for free" part of the sibling kernel is narrower than it reads. Two panels are not enough: both must be rendered AND the viewers must be fooled on different slots. With both humans fooled on the same slots there were 0 reveals, and with the lucid viewer's panel unrendered there were 0 reveals. The verb is also not a guaranteed reveal: a fooled NPC receiver took 2 verbs and exposed nothing.
- Fix: Treat the reveal as a conjunction: lucid observer AND rendered panel AND a slot where the readings differ. Where any term can be false (NPCs, off-screen split-screen, correlated hallucination), budget an explicit offer verb and make the receiver run on world truth, not on the deceived view.
- Why this fix: The model shows the verb is the only path that works when the panel term is false, and it works only if the receiver's reading is the truth. Making the receiver truth-driven is the same condition the second-actor kernel names for AI companions.
- Rule of thumb: Per-viewer derivation yields a reveal only for a slot where a lucid viewer's panel is rendered and the other viewer is fooled; in the three tested breakers (shared delusion, unrendered panel, panel-less NPC) it yields zero, so budget a verb there and make its receiver truth-driven.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand: the sandbox ran and printed RESULT PASS with 9/9 checks. The model encodes the kernel's own definitions of derivation and verb, so it confirms the logical boundary, not human play behaviour; whether players glance at a partner panel was not measured.
