## E1 — silhouette size floor survives peak and viewer metrics but not area mean
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Project: local/sandbox-silhouette-size-floor-metric-audit
- Tags: [accessibility][visualdesign][testing]
- What: Settles which pair-distance metric is defensible for the question "does silhouette-first lose to luminance-separated colour below a size floor". Re-ran the 8-slot shape cast of sandbox-silhouette-first-cue-audit/E1 against area-mean, peak-pixel and four viewer models (box resample to the physical size, opponent colour space, Gaussian low-pass in physical pixels, Minkowski pooling). Result: area-mean is indefensible (it fails a surround-invariance test and calls a plainly visible prop confusable at full size, which also makes E1's "props separate nothing, 4 of 4" an artefact), and the floor survives every defensible metric at 2 px but its depth at 4 px is set by the threshold, not by the metric family.
- Why built: tension 'Does silhouette-first lose to luminance-separated colour below a size floor, or is that floor an artefact of the distance metric?' left two metrics disagreeing (area-mean flat, peak-pixel floor at 2 px) with no basis to choose. Supports pole B of tension 'Does silhouette-first lose to luminance-separated colour below a size floor, or is that floor an artefact of the distance metric?' at 2 px only: the floor is not a metric artefact, because every metric that passes two threshold-free sanity tests shows it, while pole A (silhouette holds down to a few pixels) is what the same models show at 8 px and above and, for the lower half of the calibrated threshold window, at 4 px.
- Design: stdlib Python, no RNG, no timing. Same 16x16 canvas, masks, matched-luminance (0.28 linear) and luminance-spread palettes, and Vienot-Brettel-Mollon deutan/protan matrices as E1 (copied); count is the worst of normal, deutan, protan, greyscale. Metrics: area-mean (T 0.02), raw peak, and viewer models V1 (luminance sigma 0.5 physical px, chromatic sigma 1.25) and V2 (1.0 / 2.5) with peak, beta-4 or mean pooling, plus Weber-normalised V1-W and V2-W (difference divided by mean luminance plus glare floor 0.02, nominal 0.05). Sigmas, the 2.5x chromatic blur ratio, chromatic gain 0.5, glare floor and the 0.014 / 0.05 nominal thresholds are assumed stand-ins, not calibrated to observers. A metric counts as defensible only if (T1) a prop plainly visible at 16 px is not called confusable and (T4) padding the sprite with empty background (16 to 32 px canvas) changes the distance by under about 5 percent. Thresholds are anchored per model in a window from the distance of a 2 percent whole-body luminance change (cannot tell apart) to the distance of the visible prop pair. Casts: shape S8, luminance-spread recolour R8, hybrid H8 (shapes with spread colours), body-only B4, area-matched symmetric bodies A4 (64 px each, 16 px per quadrant).
- Got right: P1 held (replicates E1: area-mean S8 flat at 4 of 28 for 16, 8, 4, 2 px; raw peak at T 0.05 gives 0, 0, 0, 10). P2 held: area-mean prop distance 0.0065 is under its T 0.02 and its 4x-area ratio is 0.250; raw peak, V1-peak, V2-peak, V1-W, V2-W pass both tests (ratio 1.000); beta-4 pooling gives 0.707 and mean pooling 0.258, so only peak-type pooling is surround-invariant. P7 held: area-mean shows no floor anywhere in its window (0 confusable at every size), the peak-type windows are non-empty. P8 held: floor gap (count at 2 px minus count at 16 px, at least 7 of 28) is absent at the window's low end and present at half its top (raw peak 28, V1-peak 24, V2-peak 28, V1-W 26, V2-W 28 confusable at 2 px). The shape cast is never worse than the recolour at 16 px (0 of 25 thresholds) and is worse at 2 px at 60 to 100 percent of thresholds depending on the model (post-hoc X2), so the ordering "colour beats silhouette at 2 px" survives all five defensible models.
- Where/why it failed: 5 of 9 pre-registered predictions failed, so the sandbox result is FAIL. P3: at the nominal T 0.014 the 2 px count was 0 for raw peak, 4 for V1-peak and 12 for V2-peak, so a floor of 7 or more pairs in all three was wrong; only V2-peak crossed it. P4: threshold robustness over the 0.25x to 4x grid was 1, 2, 6, 0, 1 of 9 for raw-peak, V1-peak, V2-peak, V1-W, V2-W; the floor needs a threshold choice. P5: at the nominal threshold raw peak had no gap at 2 px (0 vs 0) and under V2-peak the hybrid H8 (5 pairs, 12 for shape) was worse than recolour R8 (3), so the colour-beats-silhouette gap and hybrid-no-worse parts each failed in one model. P6: area-matched symmetric bodies were 6 of 6 confusable at 2 px under every model (the strict information floor held) but stayed fully distinct at 4 px even under V2-peak (predicted 4 or more confusable), and body-only B4 became confusable only under V2-peak (3 of 6 at 2 px). P9: at the window midpoint the Weber models gave only 4 (V1-W) and 8 (V2-W) of 28 at 2 px versus 0 at 16 px, so V1-W missed the 7-pair bar. Earlier in this session an implementation slip (grey as a 3-vector inflated Euclidean distance by the square root of 3) broke the E1 replication; fixed and recorded in the header.
- Fix: do not choose between area-mean and peak by which one gives the story. Reject any metric that dilutes with empty surround (area-mean, beta-4 and mean pooling over the whole image) and validate the survivors with a positive control (a visible prop at full size). Set the threshold from two anchors, a 2 percent luminance change as the floor and a plainly visible feature as the ceiling, and report the floor as a band: under a viewer low-pass a 2 px collapse (entity about 1.25 px) shows 4 to 12 of 28 confusable pairs at the window midpoint in all five defensible models and 24 to 28 at half the window top, while 4 px shows 0 to 4 at the midpoint (4 only for V2-peak) and 2 to 16 at half the top. Compare silhouette to colour with luminance-spread recolour or a hybrid, since at coverage-limited sizes a body-matched-luminance shape cast carries only ink area and centroid.
- Why this fix: it removes the two free choices that decided E1 (which metric, which cutoff) in favour of one control each: surround invariance and anchors measured from the model's own distances. The area-matched symmetric bodies show the part that no metric can fix, because at 2 px those four bodies reduce to the same quadrant means.
- Rule of thumb: The size floor for silhouette-first is real at about 2 px per entity and is not a distance-metric artefact, but area-mean pooling hides it and also mislabels visible props as confusable, so reject area-mean and whole-image beta pooling and use peak pooling after a viewer low-pass; at 4 px the answer depends on the threshold and blur, and silhouette should be paired with luminance-separated colour for anything smaller than about 4 px per entity. Tested on 5 casts, 8 metric variants, 4 sizes and a 25-point threshold sweep.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox twice with byte-identical output (cmp). Every number above is read from that run. The viewer-model constants, thresholds, palettes and masks are invented stand-ins listed in Design, and the P7 to P9 anchors and the two sanity tests were written after seeing run 1, as the sandbox header records; the predictions P1 to P6 are unedited.
