## E1 — title matcher stutter split vs timing hyphen join
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Project: local/sandbox-title-matcher-stutter-split-vs-timing-hyphen-join
- Tags: [lyrics][songwriting][testing]
- What: A title-drop checker needs two token views of hyphens, because stutter hooks and timing hyphenation pull in opposite directions: splitting on hyphens finds stutter titles but misses words hyphenated for sung timing, and joining hyphens does the reverse.
- Why built: Compose the content-word subsequence title matcher, the stutter-hook fix (split on hyphens) and the hyphenate-to-slow kernel, which tells writers to put hyphens inside words, to see whether the stutter fix survives timing hyphenation.
- Design: Content-word subsequence matcher with a small stopword list and an -ed stemmer; three views of each line (split on hyphens, join hyphens, either). Fixture: 4 stutter lines, 4 timing-hyphenated lines (to-night, beau-ti-ful, for-ev-er, de-sire), 2 inflection variants and 3 absent-title probes.
- Got right: Split view found stutter 4/4 and timing 0/4; join view found timing 4/4 and stutter 0/4; trying both views found all 8 hyphen cases.
- Where/why it failed: My prediction of 10/10 and no false positives missed twice: the irregular past held for hold is not a hyphen problem and neither view catches it, and the split view matched the title Stay inside the compound stay-at-home, one false positive in 3 probes.
- Fix: Match a title against both the hyphen-split and hyphen-joined token views, and treat a hit that exists only inside a hyphenated compound as weaker evidence; handle irregular inflections separately.
- Why this fix: Each view alone loses a whole class (4 of 8 hyphen cases), and together they lose none; the compound false positive comes only from the split view, so it can be flagged by which view produced the hit.
- Rule of thumb: Hyphens mean opposite things in lyrics, stutter separators and timing marks inside one word, so a title matcher must try both split and joined views; either one alone missed 4 of 8 hyphenated title drops here.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox; counts are copied from its output. Whether Suno sings the hyphenated words more slowly is the kernel's claim and was not tested here.
