## E1 — new idea count needs stems and stopwords
- Date: 2026-09-30
- Project: local/sandbox-new-idea-count-needs-stems-and-stopwords
- Tags: [songwriting][lyrics][testing]
- What: Counting new content words is a poor measure of new ideas per line (off on 7 of 12 hand-labelled lines, because kitchen light or coffee cup is one idea in two words), but the one-idea kernel's coarse flag of more than three new words matched the hand overloaded label on 12 of 12.
- Why built: Compose the one-lyric-line-is-one-intonation-unit kernel with the compress-words-not-syllables lesson, to test the kernel's suggested checker and whether long-word compression trips it.
- Design: Twelve lines in three sections, each hand-labelled with its count of new ideas (a new referent, action or state). Compared a raw new-token count with a count of new content words after stopword removal and simple suffix stemming, and the over-three flag against the hand labels.
- Got right: The over-three flag agreed with the hand labels on 12 of 12 lines, and the compressed long-word lines (honestly unbelievably; eventually nevermind) scored 2 each, so the two kernels do not conflict.
- Where/why it failed: My prediction of at least 10 of 12 exact agreement for the stemmed count was wrong: it was off on 7 of 12, overcounting whenever one idea is a two-word phrase and counting after and again as content; the raw count was off on 9 of 12.
- Fix: Use the new-content-word count only as a coarse overload flag with a threshold above three, never as an exact idea count; count noun phrases instead of words if an exact figure is needed.
- Why this fix: The overcount per line was one to three, which the threshold absorbs, so the flag stays reliable while the exact count does not; a borderline three-idea line built from phrases is where it would break.
- Rule of thumb: Counting new content words overcounts ideas (off on 7 of 12 lines; kitchen light is one idea) but a more-than-three flag matched hand labels 12 of 12, so use it only as a coarse overload flag.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox; counts are copied from its output. The idea labels are my own hand judgements and another labeller could differ on a few lines.
