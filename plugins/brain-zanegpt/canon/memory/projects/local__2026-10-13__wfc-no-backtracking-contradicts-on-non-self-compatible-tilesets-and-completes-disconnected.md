## E1 — wfc no backtracking contradicts on non self compatible tilesets and completes disconnected
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Project: local/2026-10-13__wfc-no-backtracking-contradicts-on-non-self-compatible-tilesets-and-completes-disconnected
- Tags: [algorithm][gamedesign][procgen][testing][sandbox]
- What: No-backtracking wave function collapse with eager arc-consistency propagation, run on a 5x5 grid over 3,478 strided three-tile adjacency sets, counting initial failures, contradictions after a collapse, and disconnected walkable regions in completed grids.
- Why built: The audit of the eager-propagation lesson found that its 0% contradiction rate came from three hand-built tilesets; the follow-up lesson says no contradiction does not mean connected. I wanted to measure both claims over a broad enumerated space instead of three sets.
- Design: Horizontal and vertical compatibility matrices enumerated by stride; lowest-entropy-first collapse choosing the lowest tile index; propagation to a fixpoint after every collapse; tile 0 treated as walkable and flood-filled on completed grids; a subset with a self-compatible tile in both axes built to BREAK the explanation that such tilesets never contradict.
- Got right: 2,402 of 3,478 sets completed, and among them 877 (36.5 percent) left the walkable tiles disconnected, which reproduces the no-contradiction-is-not-connected warning at scale.
- Where/why it failed: Eager propagation did not prevent contradictions: 49 sets contradicted after a collapse and 1,027 failed before any collapse; and having a self-compatible tile did not rescue it, since 18 of 1,940 such sets still contradicted. The 0% of the earlier lesson is a property of its chosen tilesets, not of propagation to a fixpoint.
- Fix: Treat the contradiction rate as a measured property of each tileset: validate a tileset by enumeration or sampling before shipping, keep restart or backtracking on contradiction, and run a flood fill on completed output for connectivity.
- Why this fix: Propagation only prunes options that are locally impossible; a collapse can still choose into a dead end, and local adjacency never encodes global reachability, so only the explicit checks catch either.
- Rule of thumb: Eager propagation to a fixpoint does not make WFC contradiction-free (49 of 2,451 non-trivial sets contradicted at 5x5) and a contradiction-free result is disconnected in 36.5 percent of completed grids here; keep a restart path and a flood-fill check, and measure the rate per tileset.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox; three-tile sets on a 5x5 grid with first-option collapse only, so the rates are specific to that space and not a general frequency.
- Composed: local/sandbox-procgen-wfc/E1, local/sandbox-procgen-wfc/E2, local/sandbox-maze/E1
- Sandbox: sandboxes/2026-10-13__wfc-no-backtracking-contradicts-on-non-self-compatible-tilesets-and-completes-disconnected.py
- Prediction: Roughly a third of sets fail at initial propagation; 30 to 100 contradict after a collapse; some completed grids are disconnected; restricting to sets with a self-compatible tile reduces contradictions to 0.
- Observed: RESULT: PASS of 3478 sets: 1027 fail at init, 2402 complete (877 with disconnected walkable region), 49 contradict after a collapse; with a self-compatible tile 18 of 1940 still contradict. The self-compatible-tile prediction was refuted.
- Scenarios: 1) all strided sets, initial propagation; 2) post-collapse contradiction count; 3) flood-fill connectivity of completed grids; 4) self-compatible-tile subset, built to BREAK the explanation for the earlier 0%.
