## E1 — curing dose base meat weight vs total weight
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Project: local/curing-dose-base-meat-weight-not-total
- Tags: [curing][sandbox][numerics][model]
- What: New domain CURING seeded from a real bench scenario: dosing Cure #1 (6.25 percent sodium nitrite) for an equilibrium bag cure. Dosing 0.25 percent of the meat weight gives 156 ppm nitrite on the meat, the usual ingoing limit; dosing the same percentage on meat plus water gives 195, 234 and 312 ppm at water-to-meat ratios 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0.
- Why built: Seeding a domain with no records. The scenario is a common home-curing mistake: the recipe says percent of weight and the cook weighs the bag including brine or the meat before trimming.
- Design: Exact arithmetic: ppm on meat = cure grams x 0.0625 / meat grams x 1e6. Scenarios: correct base (156.25 ppm); total-weight base at three water ratios; wrong weighing stage with a 20 percent fat cap trimmed, once dosing on untrimmed weight and once on trimmed weight (built to BREAK the claim that the error is always an overdose). No randomness.
- Got right: The correct base reproduces the textbook 156 ppm, so the model computes the quantity from the cure strength rather than assuming it, and the overdose scales exactly as one plus the water ratio.
- Where/why it failed: My prediction treated the weighing-stage error as only an underdose; the sandbox shows it goes either way: dosing on untrimmed weight and curing trimmed meat gives 195 ppm, while dosing on trimmed weight and counting on full weight gives 125 ppm, so the sign depends on which weight the limit is defined on.
- Fix: Define the dose base as the weight of the meat actually being cured at the moment it enters the cure, never the bag total, and write the base next to the percentage in the recipe.
- Why this fix: The ppm limit is a concentration on that meat, so only meat weight at cure time makes the ratio of nitrite to meat match the number the limit quotes.
- Rule of thumb: Cure dose percentages are only meaningful with their weight base: 0.25 percent of meat weight is 156 ppm, but the same percentage of meat plus brine is 195 to 312 ppm at water ratios 0.25 to 1.0, so state the base and weigh the meat at cure time.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox for the arithmetic; the 156 ppm limit and the 6.25 percent cure strength are published figures I recalled and did not re-check against the regulation. This domain has no adjacent kernel or lesson in Brain, so the entry is isolated and cites nothing.
