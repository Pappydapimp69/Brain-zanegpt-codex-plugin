## E1 — a fixed ad kit keeps a batch above half identical even with a new joke per video
- Date: 2026-10-15
- Project: local/fake-ad-kit-template-similarity
- Tags: [game-ads][comedy][ai-video][sandbox]
- What: A batch of 8 fake ads built from the 10-asset reusable kit stays 0.625 identical per pair even when every variable slot (game name, joke, script, voiceover, layout, trope) is new, and 0.94 identical when only the game name changes. Rotating 3 of the 10 kit assets brought it to 0.48 and 6 to 0.30.
- Why built: Train edge pick: the reusable-kit kernel says reuse one asset kit so the format reads at once, the channel-policy kernel says each video needs its own joke and script or the channel looks templated, and the source-check lesson says verify a kernel's claims. This tests whether the two kernels' advice is compatible as written.
- Design: 16 feature slots per video: 10 kit assets from the kernel's list plus 6 variable slots. Similarity of a pair is the share of slots identical; batch score is the mean over all pairs of 8 videos. Kit assets either fixed or drawn from a pool of 6 variants; variable slots drawn fresh or fixed. Flag line 0.5 is an assumption, not a platform number.
- Got right: The policy kernel's warning about a game-name swap is strongly supported (0.94). Rotating even 3 kit assets moves a fresh-script batch under the line.
- Where/why it failed: The advice to put the kit first and vary only the joke and script is not enough by itself: with the kit fixed the batch is 0.625 identical, over my line, because the kit is 10 of 16 slots. The 0.5 line and equal slot weighting are assumptions; the platform's real review is not public, and kit assets are probably weighted less than script, so only the direction is a finding.
- Fix: Treat the kit as a rotating set, not a fixed one: keep the recognisable core (cursor, FAIL stamp, fake end card) stable and vary 3 or more other assets per video (jingle, timer style, character, percent text) on top of a new joke and script.
- Why this fix: The kit exists so the format reads in the first second, which needs only a few anchor cues; the rest of the kit adds sameness without adding recognition, so rotating those spends the least recognition for the most distance from a template look.
- Rule of thumb: A fixed asset kit cannot be offset by a new script alone: with 10 of 16 slots fixed a fresh-script batch was still 0.625 identical (name-swap 0.94); rotate 3 or more kit assets per video to get under 0.5.
- Provenance (verified/assumed): Verified first-hand by running the sandbox below (4 scenarios, seeded, deterministic). The slot counts, equal weighting and the 0.5 flag line are my assumptions; I did not check the platform's policy text again and did not test any real videos.
